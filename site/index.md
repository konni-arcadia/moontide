# Moontide manual

**Early:** Moontide is a young project with known bugs, and the whole Scratchpad is early experimental. The
[known limits](troubleshooting.md#known-limits) say what that means.

Moontide lets Claude play live music for you. You describe a mood, a theme or an idea. Claude
writes [Strudel](https://strudel.cc) code that plays in your browser and changes without ever
stopping.

This manual is for three kinds of reader: a musician who is starting with Claude, a musician with
MIDI gear who wants Claude to play it, and a curious reader who wants to know how it works. Every
page is written in plain words and ends with a box that says what was checked and what was not.

## Where to start

Everyone starts with the [Install](install.md) page: it adds Moontide to Claude Code or Claude
Desktop and ends with your first music. After that, pick the path that fits you.

- **A musician starting with Claude:** after [Install](install.md), read [First music](first-music.md),
  then [The page and its views](the-page.md), then [Asking Claude](asking-claude.md) to learn how to ask.
- **A musician with MIDI gear:** read [MIDI instruments and the setup file](midi-instruments.md) to describe
  your instruments, then [The Scratchpad](scratchpad.md) to play in a motif and send it to Claude.
- **A curious reader:** read [How it works](how-it-works.md), then [What was measured](what-was-measured.md).

All the pages are listed below, in order.

## What was verified

**Verified:**

- Every link between the pages, and every tool, setting, topic and number the pages name, is
  checked by a test against the code at each change.

**Not verified:**

- How the pages read in browsers other than Chrome.
- Whether a reader who has never used Moontide finds the way without help.
- The pictures in the manual come from the real page in Chrome, with the SC-55 and the keyboard set up
  as simulated devices, so the instrument did not play and nothing was heard while they were taken.
